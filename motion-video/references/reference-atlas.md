# Reference atlas

These are Oscar's supplied references. The original twelve were studied on 2026-09-04; additions record their study dates separately. Use the original films as the quality bar. The observations below are interpretation, not verified claims about the products advertised. All paths are relative to this reference directory; [library.json](library.json) records original paths, durations, dimensions, audio presence, sizes, and SHA-256 hashes.

The full visual timelines were surveyed using timestamped frames, then selected transitions at higher density. This environment could not directly ingest continuous video or listen to audio. Local speech recognition supplemented the visual study; its gaps and hallucinations are not evidence. Times below are approximate navigation aids, not frame-accurate edit points. See [study method](study-method.md). Use native playback and sound review when available in a future task.

## Motion agent: the capability to embody

[Original](videos/motion-agent.mp4) · [opening study](study/motion-agent/sheet-01.jpg) · [middle study](study/motion-agent/sheet-02.jpg) · [montage study](study/motion-agent/sheet-03.jpg) · [ending study](study/motion-agent/sheet-04.jpg) · [dense process sequence](study/motion-agent/dense-sheet.jpg) · [partial machine transcript](study/motion-agent/narration.md)

| Approximate time | What is visible | Direction for this skill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | “I am Motion” introduces a dark prompt/compositor interface that turns an idea into a video. | A short brief should initiate production. The user should not have to operate the animation tool. |
| 4–10s | Green, red, and black shapes become colored timeline tracks and keyframe diamonds. The creative-friend / After Effects description is embodied by motion. | Show capability through craft. “Unreasonably good at After Effects” describes skill and taste, not a required backend. |
| 10–17s | Pictograms, oversized words, and a contrasting serif treatment support the sleep/deadlines/font-choices joke. | Own copy, typography, timing, and personality as one composition. |
| 17–30s | “How do I work?” resolves into Prompt → Research → Plan → Build. The Build card contains Visuals, Motion, and Sound. | Research before directing; design a plan before bulk animation; complete all three crafts. |
| 30–40s | An article/URL input becomes a cream, editorial documentary treatment; subsequent examples change presentation. | Ingest meaningful context and choose a style that fits it. A URL or README is creative material, not just an asset address. |
| 40–50s | Talking-head footage is reframed and given an explanatory graphic treatment with collage, arrows, and labels. | Combine real footage with authored composition. Adapt framing and explanation to the intended format. |
| 50–54s | A selected visual receives the instruction “Change color to blue.” | Support precise natural-language revisions to an object/shot while preserving the rest of the film. |
| 54–60s | A social conversation requests an explanation and receives a video response. | Understand context supplied in ordinary language. This does not authorize sending messages or posting outputs. |
| 60–73s | A black-hole/spacetime mesh and formula animate an explanation; the prompt explicitly asks for visual mathematical storytelling. | Animate the mechanism itself. For product films, show what changes and why, rather than decorating a feature label. |
| 73–86s | NASA/Artemis diagrams, labeled rocket hardware, orbital composition, and a TP-7 product drawing. | Use diagrams, illustration, and precise layouts when those best communicate the idea. |
| 87–103s | Tesla editorial graphics, Egyptian card-game imagery, a luminous pixel heart, and an architectural illustration. | Range matters. The artist chooses visual language; there is no mandatory house gradient, font, or card layout. |
| 104–116s | A YouTube origin/year card develops into a dimensional tower of red play-button blocks. | Build transformations around a persistent meaningful object, with a clear silhouette and payoff. |
| 117–124s | Motion / by Mosaic / motion.so branding settles for the ending, followed by black at the final frame. | Resolve the film and let the identity remain readable; make any fade/tail deliberate. |

The reusable workflow is **context → research → treatment/storyboard → visuals + motion + sound → render → inspect → targeted revision**. The film's marketing promises do not establish installed tools, verified features, free access, or guaranteed agency-quality output from one prompt. The skill makes the workflow explicit and keeps Astra accountable for inspecting the result.

## Use this formula: the preferred structure

[Original](videos/use-this-formula.mp4) · [opening](study/use-this-formula/sheet-01.jpg) · [middle](study/use-this-formula/sheet-02.jpg) · [proof/payoff](study/use-this-formula/sheet-03.jpg) · [close](study/use-this-formula/sheet-04.jpg) · [dense demo](study/use-this-formula/dense-sheet.jpg) · [machine transcript](study/use-this-formula/narration.md)

The Zaro film has an annotation ruler with **Dream outcome, Solution, Demo, Outro**. Those are the reference's actual labels. The ruler is an explanatory overlay; do not put it in a new finished film unless asked.

| Approximate time | Observed construction |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5s | The desired outcome is immediate: apps and agents from one prompt. |
| 5–9s | Product introduction and logo make the solution explicit. |
| 9–20s | A sentence becomes a client/deal app with recognizable table and pipeline states. |
| 20–28s | Agents are added; cards, deal updates, and charts make their contribution visible. |
| 28–33s | “Simple to start” and “Powerful to scale” are paired with the prompt and a richer workflow graph. |
| 33–44s | Dispersed files/tools resolve into organized context and connections. |
| 44–55s | A question becomes a sourced answer; closeups allow the useful output to be read. |
| 55–59s | The work travels into a phone/chat context. |
| 59–65s | A system-level payoff reinterprets the initial app-building promise. |
| 65–68.6s | Logo, address, and next step settle into the close. |

The center of gravity is the demonstration. Short benefit copy alternates with visible cause and effect. A consistent cream/dark/lavender system lets composition and motion vary without losing identity. The longer version expands the idea, not just the number of feature labels.

For 10/20/30 seconds, preserve the logic rather than proportionally squeezing this 69-second timeline. A 10-second cut can combine outcome and solution in one shot, demonstrate one transformation, and land the close. Remove a secondary idea before shrinking readable holds.

## Use storyboard: composition precedes choreography

[Original](videos/use-storyboard.mp4) · [opening](study/use-storyboard/sheet-01.jpg) · [demo](study/use-storyboard/sheet-02.jpg) · [ending](study/use-storyboard/sheet-03.jpg) · [dense input sequence](study/use-storyboard/dense-sheet.jpg)

This is a Jurni film presented beneath a storyboard strip and moving playhead. The presentation format itself teaches the process; it is not a required aspect ratio or final-video wrapper.

- **0–9s:** a pixel/stair gradient introduces the mark, followed by a field of ecommerce pages.
- **9–17s:** “One Agentic Engine” and “Every Shopping Journey” connect a central icon to capabilities; a thinking pill becomes an icon/wordmark.
- **18–31s:** the camera enters the actual app, isolates a funnel request, approaches the send control, then returns to the conversation.
- **31–45s:** visible completion states lead to a page-builder/product-page result, supporting the context-to-live-funnel promise.
- **45–53s:** a published/success state resolves to the brand close.

The storyboard establishes framing, transformation, and sequence before the final animation. Use designed stills with timed transition notes. A prose list of “show dashboard; zoom; reveal logo” misses the lesson. When a closeup completes an action, return to enough context to understand its consequence.

## Short films

### Quick teaser — Inspo, 10.09s

[Original](videos/quick-teaser.mp4) · [overview](study/quick-teaser/sheet-01.jpg) · [dense selection sequence](study/quick-teaser/dense-sheet.jpg)

At **0–1.7s**, a concrete landing-page request sets the argument. At **1.8–3.8s**, a large website-reference mosaic conveys discovery and range. At **3.8–4.8s**, selection marks, background de-emphasis, and an overlapping stack make the choice graspable. At **4.8–7.5s**, isolated page examples become the visual proof. At **8–10s**, the cream field, serif Inspo mark, red dot, and coming-soon line provide a quiet close.

Learn the continuous **request → field → selection → quality result → brand** argument. The speed comes from removing navigation and carrying the same objects through transitions. Do not imitate only the page-wall effect while losing the reason for it.

### 10-second reference — Bytebrew asset family, 9.96s

[Original](videos/reference-10s.mp4) · [overview](study/reference-10s/sheet-01.jpg) · [dense grid-to-hero sequence](study/reference-10s/dense-sheet.jpg)

At **0–3s**, a warm cream, coral, navy, and pixel-type brand system appears across a book/grid of outputs. At **3.5–7.3s**, a video grid expands and promotes a coffee-pouring image to the hero. At **7.5–10s**, an ecommerce page carries the same visual identity and a dimensional coral bean.

Learn consistent art direction across outputs, appetizing material treatment, and grid-to-hero continuity. The source has **no audio stream** and remains visually coherent. The on-screen generation watermark and brand text are not production assets or independent proof of tool capabilities.

### 12-second reference — Nano Banana Pro, 12.35s

[Original](videos/reference-12s.mp4) · [overview](study/reference-12s/sheet-01.jpg) · [dense type/material sequence](study/reference-12s/dense-sheet.jpg)

At **5.7–6.9s**, rapid banana treatments change material and style. At **7–12s**, an input box and uploaded items lead into an image-combination demonstration.

### 15-second reference — BLACKBOX.AI, 14.98s

[Original](videos/reference-15s.mp4) · [overview](study/reference-15s/sheet-01.jpg) · [dense opening](study/reference-15s/dense-sheet.jpg)

## Longer product demonstrations

### Apple-style — 22.11s

[Original](videos/apple-style.mp4) · [opening](study/apple-style/sheet-01.jpg) · [ending](study/apple-style/sheet-02.jpg) · [dense result sequence](study/apple-style/dense-sheet.jpg)

At **0–5s**, tiny copy, enormous cropped type, and colored role tags create scale contrast. At **6–13s**, a single launch-video prompt is followed by ordered work states including graphics, sound, and style. At **13–20s**, a preview becomes the focal point, with role cursors and palette changes making the result appear editable. At **20–22s**, the scene resolves to a sparse identity frame.

Learn negative space, one focal point, crisp hierarchy, and visible editability. Small labels may not survive a mobile feed; reassess them for the new cut. The filename and on-screen Apple reference do not make this a verified Apple advertisement or prescribe Apple assets.

### 37-second reference — Polymarket Perps depiction, 37.18s

[Original](videos/reference-37s.mp4) · [opening](study/reference-37s/sheet-01.jpg) · [payoff](study/reference-37s/sheet-02.jpg) · [dense card-to-order transition](study/reference-37s/dense-sheet.jpg)

At **0–9s**, the always-open-market promise leads through navigation to the product name. At **9–19s**, an angled dashboard isolates an instrument card, then the order form: amount, leverage, risk controls, and action. At **20–30s**, a live-position view and broader instrument range show the result and scope. At **31–37s**, the address and brand get a generous hold.

Learn cinematic framing that yields to legibility during the decisive interaction, controlled light sweeps, and an action-to-result chain. A polished financial-product reference is not validation of its factual or performance claims.

### 45-second reference — GitHub sync depiction, 45.16s

[Original](videos/reference-45s.mp4) · [opening](study/reference-45s/sheet-01.jpg) · [middle](study/reference-45s/sheet-02.jpg) · [close](study/reference-45s/sheet-03.jpg) · [dense push transition](study/reference-45s/dense-sheet.jpg)

At **0–14s**, the feature is named and its import control is located in the app. At **15–26s**, changed files and a push action become a branch icon, a circular brand mark, an expanding colored aperture, and a confirmation check. At **27–40s**, the reverse operation is explained and demonstrated: pull, progress, success. At **41–45s**, the CTA closes the loop.

Learn the alternation **functional control → concise language → abstract transformation → functional confirmation**. The vivid gradient occupies a motivated passage in a controlled system. Preserve the feature's causal explanation when making a more restrained treatment.

### 50-second reference — Shipper, 49.86s

[Original](videos/reference-50s.mp4) · [opening](study/reference-50s/sheet-01.jpg) · [middle](study/reference-50s/sheet-02.jpg) · [close](study/reference-50s/sheet-03.jpg) · [dense team/cost sequence](study/reference-50s/dense-sheet.jpg)

At **0–17s**, rotating team roles lead into a prompt and recognizable research/design/work states. At **17–24s**, one avatar expands into a team and a rising cost counter, then contracts into the Shipper circle through concentric rings. At **24–44s**, actual chat/tasks and output connect the economic proposition to the product. At **45–50s**, the brand and a build prompt echo the opening interaction.

Learn one persistent circular motif, a coherent mint palette with selective orange, and repeated returns from abstraction to evidence. Do not inherit “free,” ranking, salary, or million-dollar comparisons without product-specific support.

### One-minute reference — Firecrawl, 66.15s

[Original](videos/reference-1m.mp4) · [opening](study/reference-1m/sheet-01.jpg) · [middle](study/reference-1m/sheet-02.jpg) · [mechanism](study/reference-1m/sheet-03.jpg) · [ending](study/reference-1m/sheet-04.jpg) · [dense square-to-globe sequence](study/reference-1m/dense-sheet.jpg)

At **0–15s**, square/grid geometry connects a question, source material, and extracted facts. At **16–25s**, orange planes fold into a cube, dissolve into a square field, and become a globe/grid with orbiting nodes. At **26–39s**, websites, wireframes, HTML, and a selected page-to-JSON transformation repeatedly explain the mechanism. At **40–54s**, structured documents and filtered product cards reinforce it. At **55–66s**, a connection hub and brand close broaden the payoff.

Learn how a limited grammar—orange, squares, grids, paths—can sustain a long film through real transformations and varied shot scale. The motif explains unstructured web material becoming usable structure. It is not just decoration between slides. Treat customer-count and integration claims as unverified reference copy.

## External toolbox, subordinate to the director

[PixelFrame](https://www.pixelframe.co/) provides a useful agency-process reference: understand brand/audience, develop storyboard and visual direction, then animate and finish. Take responsibility for those jobs; do not equate choosing a renderer with doing them.

[Remotion's official agent skills](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/ai/skills) are implementation guidance. [Pixabay app sound effects](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/app/) are a possible source for a specific cue. Consult current documentation, terms, and asset pages when actually using them. Neither selects the concept or certifies the finished film.
